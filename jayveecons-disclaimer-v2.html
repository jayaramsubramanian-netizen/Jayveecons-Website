Privacy Policy
How Jayveecons Industries Pvt. Ltd. collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through this website
Overview

Jayveecons Industries Pvt. Ltd. is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who interact with this website. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and your rights in relation to it.

This website is an information and enquiry platform for an engineering and manufacturing company. We do not operate an online store, user accounts, subscription service, or any platform requiring ongoing personal data processing. Our data collection is limited to what is necessary to respond to legitimate business enquiries and employment applications.

By using this website or submitting an enquiry through it, you consent to the collection and use of your personal information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you actively provide it to us. The primary ways this occurs are:

Contact and project enquiries: When you submit a Request for Quotation (RFQ) or general enquiry through our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, and the details of your project or equipment requirement. This information is used solely to evaluate and respond to your enquiry.

Career applications: When you submit a CV or job application via email to hr@jayveecons.com, we collect the personal and professional information contained in your CV and covering communication. This is used only to assess your suitability for roles at Jayveecons and is handled by our HR team.

Email correspondence: If you contact us directly by email, we retain those communications for the purpose of responding to and following up on your enquiry.

Website usage data: Our website hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages) may collect standard server log data including IP addresses, browser type, referring URLs, and page visit timestamps for security and performance monitoring. This data is not linked to individually identifiable persons and is not used for marketing or profiling.

Information We Do Not Collect

To be explicit about the limited scope of our data collection, Jayveecons does not collect or process:

Payment card numbers, bank account details, or any financial information — we do not process online transactions. Sensitive personal information including health data, identity document numbers, or biometric data. Location data beyond the country or city you voluntarily provide in an enquiry. Data from social media platforms or advertising networks. Behavioural tracking data for remarketing or targeted advertising purposes.

We do not use third-party advertising networks, tracking pixels, or behavioural analytics tools on this website.

How We Use Your Information

Responding to enquiries: Information submitted via our contact form or by email is used to evaluate your project requirements, prepare a technical or commercial response, and follow up on the enquiry. This is the primary and predominant use of any personal data we receive.

Career applications: CV and application data is reviewed by our HR team and relevant department heads to assess candidate suitability. Unsuccessful applications may be retained for up to six months in the event a suitable position arises. Applications are not shared with third parties.

Business communications: With your implicit consent through initiating contact, we may send follow-up communications related to your specific enquiry. We do not send unsolicited marketing communications.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with third parties for commercial purposes. We do not use enquiry data to build marketing profiles or lists.

Data Storage and Security

Enquiry and correspondence data is stored in Jayveecons' internal business systems, accessible only to relevant sales, engineering, and management personnel on a need-to-know basis. Career application data is handled by our HR team.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or misuse. These include access controls, secure email handling, and standard professional data management practices appropriate to a business of our size and nature.

While we take these precautions, no data transmission over the internet or electronic storage system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to us electronically, and you do so at your own risk.

Retention: Project enquiry data is retained for as long as the business relationship or potential business relationship is active, and for a reasonable period thereafter to support any follow-up or dispute resolution. Career applications are retained for up to six months from receipt if unsuccessful. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Your Rights below).

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies that collect personally identifiable information.

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may set security-related cookies (such as a challenge cookie) as part of its standard DDoS protection and performance infrastructure. These are functional and security cookies that do not identify you personally and are not used for marketing purposes. They are set by Cloudflare's infrastructure, not by Jayveecons.

If we introduce any additional cookies in future, this policy will be updated and you will be notified through a notice on the website.

Your Rights

In relation to personal information you have provided to Jayveecons, you have the right to:

Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

Correction: Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete information we hold.

Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to any legal or legitimate business obligations we have to retain it.

Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent to further communication at any time by notifying us in writing. This will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at sales@jayveecons.com or by post at our Bengaluru office. We will respond to requests within 30 days.
